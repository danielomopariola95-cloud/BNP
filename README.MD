## Project 1: Revenue Forecasting System

**File:** `BNP`

Predicts future revenue using time-series machine learning. Designed for investment banking scenarios where accurate revenue forecasts are critical for financial planning and risk assessment.

### Key Features
- **Chronological train/test split** - trains on 80% historical data, tests on newest 20% (no look-ahead bias - critical for finance)
- **Feature importance analysis** - shows exactly which factors drive revenue
- **Professional visualizations** - Bloomberg-style charts
- **Multiple performance metrics** - MAE, R-squared, Accuracy

### How to Run
1. Open `BNP` in Google Colab
2. Click "Run All"
3. View the forecasts and metrics

### Results
- **95%+ accuracy** on synthetic data
- **MAE under £5** (on average, predictions are off by less than £5)
- **Seasonality identified as top revenue driver**

## Project 2: Customer Churn Prediction

**File:** `churn`

Predicts which customers are likely to leave (churn). Used for customer retention strategies in banking, telecom, and subscription services.

### Key Features
- **Random Forest classification** with 100 decision trees
- **5 performance metrics** - Accuracy, Precision, Recall, F1 Score, ROC AUC
- **Confusion matrix** - shows exactly where the model gets confused
- **Feature importance** - shows what drives customer churn

### How to Run
1. Open `churn` in Google Colab
2. Click "Run All"
3. View the predictions and insights

### Results
- **~80% accuracy**
- **Tenure and contract type** are the top predictors of churn
- **Good balance** between catching churners and avoiding false alarms
